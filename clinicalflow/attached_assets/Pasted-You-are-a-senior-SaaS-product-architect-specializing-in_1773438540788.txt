You are a senior SaaS product architect specializing in healthcare platforms.

Design and implement a professional marketing landing page for the ClinicFlow platform similar to modern doctor booking platforms.

The page must attract three types of users:

Patients
Doctors
Clinics

Important constraints:

• Do NOT break the existing SaaS architecture
• Use existing doctors, clinics, and appointment data
• Keep the design modern and mobile responsive
• Ensure fast loading performance
