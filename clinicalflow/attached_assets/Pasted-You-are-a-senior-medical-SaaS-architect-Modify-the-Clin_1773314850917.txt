You are a senior medical SaaS architect.

Modify the ClinicFlow patient record system to protect doctor-to-doctor privacy while still allowing medical collaboration within the same specialty.

Important design goal:

Doctors must NOT see which doctor previously treated the patient.

However, they should still be able to access relevant lab results and radiology within their own specialty.

---

1. Hide Previous Doctor Identity

---

In the patient medical history timeline:

Do NOT display the name of previous doctors.

Instead show only:

visit_date
specialty
visit_type

Example:

Previous Visit
Specialty: Cardiology
Date: 12 Mar 2026

Doctor name must NOT be visible to other doctors.

---

2. Prescription Privacy

---

Doctors can ONLY see prescriptions they personally created.

Condition:

prescriptions.doctor_id = current_doctor_id

Doctors cannot view prescriptions created by other doctors.

---

3. Lab Results Visibility Rules

---

Doctors may view lab results uploaded by other doctors only if:

lab_tests.specialty_id = doctor.specialty_id

However:

Do NOT display the doctor who requested the test.

Visible fields:

test_name
result_file
upload_date
specialty

Hidden fields:

ordering_doctor_name
ordering_doctor_id

---

4. Radiology Visibility Rules

---

Radiology files follow the same rule.

Doctors may view radiology uploaded by doctors within the same specialty.

Condition:

radiology.specialty_id = doctor.specialty_id

Do NOT display:

doctor_name
doctor_id

Only display:

scan_type
upload_date
specialty
file

---

5. Patient Timeline Privacy Mode

---

Patient timeline should display events without revealing doctor identities.

Example:

12 Mar 2026
Cardiology Consultation

10 Mar 2026
Cardiology Lab Test

8 Mar 2026
Cardiology Radiology

Doctor names must remain hidden.

---

6. Admin Override

---

Admins can see full details including:

doctor_name
doctor_id

because admin requires full operational visibility.

---

## Goal

Maintain patient medical continuity while preventing doctor-to-doctor visibility to avoid professional sensitivity and preserve privacy.
