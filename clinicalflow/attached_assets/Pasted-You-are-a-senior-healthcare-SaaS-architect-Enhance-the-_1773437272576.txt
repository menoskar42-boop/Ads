Enhance the ClinicFlow system by improving the Doctor Profile Page with two conversion-focused features:

1. Nearest Available Appointment Display (with clinic location)
2. AI Assistant to help patients decide if the doctor is suitable for their symptoms.

Important constraints:

• Do NOT redesign the whole system
• Preserve existing appointment and scheduling logic
• Support doctors working in multiple clinics

FEATURE 1
Nearest Available Appointments with Location
--------------------------------------------

Add a section to the doctor profile page that displays the doctor's nearest available appointment slots.

Doctors may work in multiple clinics or medical centers.

Therefore each appointment slot must include:

Doctor Name
Clinic Name
Clinic Address
Appointment Date
Appointment Time
Visit Type (Consultation / Follow-up / Urgent)

Example display:

Address: Nasr City

Address: Heliopolis

Tomorrow
4:40 PM

Each slot should include a direct booking button.

Example:

Book Appointment

The system must fetch available slots from all clinics where the doctor works.

Slots must be ordered by:

appointment_date
appointment_time

## MULTI-CLINIC SUPPORT

Doctors may belong to multiple clinics.

Appointment slots must include:

clinic_id
clinic_name
clinic_address

When showing available slots, the system must merge schedules from all clinics associated with the doctor.

FEATURE 2
AI Assistant for Doctor Suitability
-----------------------------------

Add a button on the doctor profile page:

"Is this doctor suitable for my condition?"

When clicked, open a small chat interface.

The patient can describe symptoms.

Example input:

"I have chest pain"
"I feel knee pain"
"I have severe headache"

The AI should analyze the symptoms and compare them with the doctor's specialty.

AI responses should follow these rules:

If the symptoms match the doctor's specialty:

Respond that the doctor can help with this condition.

Then display the nearest available appointment slots.

Example response:

"These symptoms are often treated by cardiology specialists.
Dr Ahmed Hassan can help you with this condition."

Show appointment slots and a booking button.

---

If the symptoms do NOT match the doctor's specialty:

Suggest a more suitable specialty.

Example:

"These symptoms may be better evaluated by a Neurology specialist."

Optionally display recommended doctors in that specialty.

---

## AI SAFETY RULE

The AI must NOT provide medical diagnosis.

It should only guide patients toward the appropriate specialty.
