# Salla plans — official pricing

- **URL:** https://salla.com/en/plans
- **Accessed:** 2026-08-26
- **Source tier:** 1 (vendor-owned official pricing page)
- **Market:** Saudi Arabia / GCC

## Captured facts

The page presents a free **Basic** plan for new sellers. It says Basic supports unlimited products and orders, customer-base building, promotions, reviews/Q&A, and discounted Salla shipping labels. The page shows **Salla Plus at SAR 990 annually** (the page also displays an annual-equivalent figure of 11.88, which appears to be a rendering issue and should not be interpreted as a monthly price without confirmation). Plus includes Basic, online payments, local and international shipping, custom domain, product types, and selling across branches with Salla Point for free. The page shows **Salla Pro at SAR 2,990 annually** (and a displayed annual figure of 3,588); the complete Pro feature list should be confirmed directly with Salla before quoting.

The page claims “over 68,000 active stores.” The pricing selector says annual billing is 16% off. Taxes, payment-processing fees, country eligibility, POS limits, and WhatsApp/CRM capabilities are not established by this page.

## Research use

Salla is a strong Saudi commerce/order/branch benchmark, but it is not evidence that a single product provides a full WhatsApp CRM, booking, inventory, and follow-up workflow. Treat prices as published annual prices, not OscarDevs assumptions.
