## Track opportunities easily in the sales software

### Your pipeline at a glance.

In our Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system, each opportunity is listed as a card with all the essential information. Each stage gives an overview of your expected revenues.

![](https://odoocdn.com/openerp_website/static/src/img/apps/crm/separator.svg)

### Efficient organization

The Kanban view organizes opportunities by stage. Drag and drop them on the pipeline to move them from one stage to another.

## Never miss out on a follow-up

**Schedule calls, meetings, mailings, or quotations** in a few clicks in the contact management software. Odoo automatically plans the next activity based on your sales script.

![](https://odoocdn.com/openerp_website/static/src/img/apps/crm/follow-up-example.svg)

## Effortless communication  means no miscommunication

**Communication is key to enhancing customer relationships.** Incoming emails are automatically added to your pipeline, and all contact with your team and customers is made from a single place, ensuring easy access to information at all times.
