# ZATCA e-invoicing

- **URL:** http://zatca.gov.sa/en/E-Invoicing/Pages/default.aspx
- **Accessed:** 2026-08-26
- **Source tier:** 1 (Saudi government authority)
- **Market:** Saudi Arabia

## Captured facts

ZATCA describes e-invoicing as converting paper invoices and notes into an electronic process that permits exchange and processing of invoices, credit notes, and debit notes in a structured electronic format. The page states that Saudi Arabia’s Fatoorah implementation has **two phases** and links to preparation, laws/regulations, FAQs, and educational material. It identifies itself as a Saudi government website and explains that official Saudi government URLs end in `gov.sa`.

## Compliance implications

Any Saudi SME product that creates invoices/orders or integrates POS should support a ZATCA-compatible e-invoicing path, immutable audit data, and a clear implementation/versioning plan. This page alone does not establish every technical field, deadline, taxpayer threshold, cloud-hosting requirement, or API behavior; those claims require the linked ZATCA technical guidance and the customer's tax adviser.
