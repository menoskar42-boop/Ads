### Are WhatsApp fees included in the respond.io subscription?

WhatsApp fees are not included in any respond.io subscription. Meta charges these fees based on your usage.

You can top up your WhatsApp credit on our platform for WhatsApp calls or messages.

Check out our [WhatsApp Business API Pricing Calculator](https://respond.io/whatsapp-pricing-calculator) for an estimate of how much your messages can cost or contact us for the latest WhatsApp call rates.

### What is a Monthly Active Contact?

A Monthly Active contact is someone you have talked to over calls or chat during the billing month. If a contact messages or calls your business or you initiate a conversation with them, they count as a Monthly Active Contact for that month.

We believe our pricing should reflect the value we deliver to you. Chats with contacts are opportunities to increase revenue and Monthly Active Contact pricing ensures you only pay for the contacts you talk to.

### What if I reach my Monthly Active Contact limit?

If you exceed your Monthly Active Contact limit, your operations will not be interrupted. Instead, you will be billed on demand for any additional contacts in increments of 100 Monthly Active Contacts. You will be charged $12 per 100 MACs on Growth and $15 per 100 MACs on Advanced. This charge will be applied at the end of your billing cycle. Please note on-demand Monthly Active Contacts are intended for temporary, seasonal usage spikes. If you require a permanent increase, upgrading your plan is more cost-effective.

### How do AI Credits work?

Every plan that includes AI features comes with a generous allocation of AI Credits. Credits power features like AI Agents, AI Assist and AI Summary, and your allocation grows automatically with your Monthly Active Contact tier. 99% of businesses never exceed their included credits. [Learn more.](https://respond.io/help/organization-settings/ai-credits)

### What happens if I exceed my AI Credits?

Your AI features won't stop. On current paid plans, AI credit on-demand activates automatically. Your AI features and workflows keep running without interruption at $15 per 1,000 additional credits. A hard cap at 200% of your total allowance ensures your costs stay predictable. [Learn more.](https://respond.io/help/organization-settings/ai-credits)

### How much do AI Agents cost on respond.io?

AI usage is included at **no extra cost** on our self-service Growth, Advanced and Enterprise plans. To prevent misuse, we have a fair use policy with generous limits that 99% of users will never reach so you can use AI without worrying about unexpected charges.

### How does the free trial work?

Our 7-day free trial provides immediate access to all features of the Growth plan for 5 users and 1,000 Monthly Active Contacts, except for broadcasts. Please note that certain features, such as topping up your WhatsApp Business Account, require a subscription. If you need more time to explore respond.io, please [contact our support team](https://respond.io/contact) to discuss extending your trial.

### What payment forms do you accept?

We accept most major credit cards including Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover. We also offer alternative payment methods, such as bank transfer for high volume customers. [Talk to sales](https://respond.io/demo) to learn more.

### Can I upgrade or cancel my plan at any time?

Yes, you can cancel or upgrade your subscription at any time. To cancel, go to the Billing section of the Platform, and click Cancel Subscription. You will continue to receive the features and limits of your plan until the end of the billing cycle. To upgrade, go to the billing section of the Platform, select a plan and click Subscribe or you can [talk to sales](https://respond.io/demo).

### Are volume discounts available?

Yes, we provide volume discounts for customers on our Growth and Advanced Plans with high Monthly Active Contact volumes or User counts. Feel free to [get in touch with sales](https://respond.io/demo) to explore a customized package that fits your needs.

### What does the 28 Days Onboarding Program include?

Embark on a 28-day guided journey with our Onboarding Specialists. Designed for B2C companies aiming to enhance revenue from inbound leads over WhatsApp and other instant messaging channels, our program ensures a seamless migration to respond.io with tailored support every step of the way. Subject to availability.

### How does respond.io protect my data?

Respond.io prioritizes your data security through a combination of encryption, access controls and security audits. Our ISO 27001 certification further highlights our commitment to maintaining top-tier information security standards. For more insights into our security measures, visit our security page.
