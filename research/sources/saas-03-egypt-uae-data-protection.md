- **Source tier:** 1 (Egyptian data-protection authority and UAE legislation portal)
- **Markets:** Egypt and UAE

## Egypt — PDPC

The Egyptian Personal Data Protection Center says it is the national authority responsible for enforcing the Personal Data Protection Law and its Executive Regulations No. 816 of 2025. It identifies Law No. 151 of 2020 and those regulations as the legal framework, covering collection, processing, storage, use, and transfer. The site says its services include licenses and permits and specialist technical/legal consultations. Its FAQ says the law regulates electronic personal data of Egyptian citizens inside or outside Egypt and non-Egyptian citizens residing in Egypt.

**Product implication:** WhatsApp contact records, booking details, order history, staff/customer identifiers, and follow-up logs should have purpose limitation, access controls, retention/deletion processes, data-subject request handling, processor/vendor contracts, and an assessed cross-border transfer posture. Licensing/permit applicability must be checked with Egyptian counsel and the PDPC.

## UAE — Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021

The UAE legislation portal identifies Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 concerning protection of personal data, with issue date 20 September 2021 and effective date 2 January 2022. The legislation includes data-subject rights including transfer, correction/erasure, restricting processing, and stopping processing.

**Product implication:** A UAE deployment needs a privacy notice, lawful processing basis/consent design where applicable, rights workflow, processor/subprocessor transparency, security controls, retention rules, and cross-border transfer review. Free-zone or sector-specific regimes may also apply; do not represent this federal law as the only UAE requirement.
