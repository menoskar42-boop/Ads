# Vertical SaaS research for Arabic SMEs

**Research date:** 2026-08-26  
**Scope:** Egypt, Saudi Arabia, UAE; WhatsApp CRM, booking, orders, inventory, branches, and follow-up.

## Key facts

* Salla publishes a free Basic tier, SAR 990/year Plus, and SAR 2,990/year Pro on its official Saudi-facing plans page. It explicitly mentions unlimited products/orders on Basic and branch selling through Salla Point on Plus.
* Foodics is a regional restaurant/POS operator with Egypt, Saudi, UAE and other country contact routes. Its UAE pricing result says a quote is built around hardware and software; no public price was verified. This is a sales-led restaurant benchmark rather than a published price.
* ZATCA’s official page says Saudi Fatoorah e-invoicing exchanges structured invoices/credit/debit notes and rolls out in two phases.
* Egypt’s PDPC identifies Law 151/2020 and Executive Regulations 816/2025; UAE’s legislation portal identifies Federal Decree-Law 45/2021, effective 2 January 2022. Both matter when CRM stores WhatsApp identity, orders, bookings, or staff information.
* G2 and Capterra are useful comparative directories, but their rankings/reviews are not evidence of Arabic localization, local tax compliance, or current price. Vendor pages and regulators outrank directories.

## Gaps and product opportunity

1. **One Arabic workflow:** Most alternatives specialize in commerce, restaurant POS, booking, accounting, or messaging. A branch-aware timeline joining WhatsApp lead → booking/order → payment/invoice → stock → automated follow-up is a credible gap.
2. **Local compliance as a feature:** Saudi ZATCA-ready invoices, Egypt PDPC controls, UAE federal-law controls, consent/opt-out logs, retention and export/delete tooling, and Arabic audit trails should be first-class.
3. **Low-friction branch operations:** Offline-friendly staff workflows, Arabic/English templates, role-based branch permissions, shared inbox assignment, deduplication of phone numbers, and owner-level rollups are often the difference between a CRM demo and daily adoption.
4. **Transparent regional pricing:** Salla is a useful benchmark because it publishes Saudi prices; many restaurant, ERP, and messaging competitors sell by quote or add usage/API fees. A clear EGP/SAR/AED plan with WhatsApp usage shown separately can reduce buyer uncertainty.
5. **Vertical packs:** Beauty/clinic appointment reminders, restaurant order/status flows, retail reorder alerts, and service-business callbacks should be separate templates built on one core.

* Confirm ZATCA phase deadlines, technical invoice fields, cryptographic/device requirements, and whether the selected integration is an approved/compatible solution.
* Confirm Egypt PDPC permits, executive-regulation operational requirements, and UAE federal versus free-zone/sector rules with counsel.
* Market-size and adoption estimates from commercial reports and directory review counts should be treated as directional until independently cross-checked.
