# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing pages on the main OscarDevs site (`/`, `/about`, `/contact`, `/faq`, `/our-work`, `/apply`)
- Public blog index and article pages (`/blog`, `/blog/:slug`)
- Public tenant landing pages reachable via canonical subdomains or `/view/:slug` previews
- Public tenant commerce/content detail pages that are meant to be indexed (for example `/shop/:slug/product/:id`)
- Public auxiliary subdomain apps shipped from this repo when they are intended for unauthenticated users (`mykid.*`, `adhd.*`)
- Crawlability control files (`robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`, manifest/icons)

## Out of scope
- Authenticated company dashboard routes (`/company/**`)
- Super admin routes (`/admin/**`)
- Authenticated or transactional customer routes (`/customer/**`)
- Checkout, cart, order confirmation, order tracking, and other routes explicitly marked `noindex`
- Demo/sample tenant surfaces intentionally excluded from indexing by source rules

## Target audience
- Arabic-speaking small and medium businesses in Egypt and the wider Arab market looking for portfolio sites, online stores, or vertical business websites.
