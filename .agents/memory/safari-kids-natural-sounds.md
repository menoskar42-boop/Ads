---
name: Safari Kids natural sounds
description: Source, playback, and attribution rules for wildlife recordings.
---

Use only distinct recordings whose public source clearly matches the animal; do not reuse one sound as if it represents multiple species. Keep the existing spoken name behavior, make natural audio user-initiated, and show the recording source and license in the activity.

**Why:** Natural recordings add value only when the sound is identifiable and rights are clear; vague or repeated sounds can teach children the wrong association.

**How to apply:** Prefer public metadata-backed sources such as Wikimedia Commons. Leave animals without a suitable recording without a sound button, and stop any currently playing recording before another starts or the activity exits.
