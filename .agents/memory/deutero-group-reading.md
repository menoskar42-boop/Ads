---
name: Deuterocanonical group reading
description: Source-of-truth rule for deuterocanonical chapters opened from group daily readings
---

Deuterocanonical chapters in group daily reading must use the verified St-Takla source path, with an explicit source-unavailable state instead of silently showing an incomplete local copy.

**Why:** The local apocrypha content is incomplete for several books and can make a scheduled card open with no verses even though St-Takla has the full chapter.

**How to apply:** When adding or changing a group reader, resolve the book through the St-Takla catalog and fetch the requested chapter before rendering or allowing completion.
