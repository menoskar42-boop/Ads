---
name: Deuterocanonical group reading
description: Source-of-truth rule for deuterocanonical chapters opened from group daily readings
---

Deuterocanonical chapters in group daily reading must use the verified St-Takla source path, with an explicit source-unavailable state instead of silently showing an incomplete local copy.

When checking the saved 1877 Arabic text against St-Takla, preserve the source's verse numbering exactly. Sirach 29 currently jumps from verse 14 to 19 on the live page; do not infer or add verses 15–18.

**Why:** The local apocrypha content is incomplete for several books and can make a scheduled card open with no verses even though St-Takla has the full chapter.

**How to apply:** When adding or changing a group reader, resolve the book through the St-Takla catalog and fetch the requested chapter before rendering or allowing completion. When auditing the saved full-book data, compare the exact live verse sequence and keep any gaps that are present in St-Takla itself.
